# EKSU Digital Clearance & Course Form Signing System

This system is a university document workflow platform built to replace manual, paper-based student clearance and course registration approvals. It helps students upload required forms, allows staff to review and sign documents, and gives administrators control over the whole approval process.

## What the system does

### 1. Student workflow
Students can:
- register and login to the student portal
- complete their profile by uploading a passport and signature
- upload their clearance form
- view the status of the clearance process
- track approvals from the Bursar and Auditor
- register courses and follow the HOD approval process
- download the signed PDF after approval

### 2. Staff workflow
Staff members can:
- login based on their role
- review student documents assigned to them
- approve or reject files with a reason
- sign documents digitally
- track document history and status updates

### 3. Admin workflow
Administrators can:
- manage staff and student records
- approve or reject staff registrations
- set clearance periods for when students can submit their forms
- monitor system health and document progress
- view audits and status reports

### 4. Admin login flow
The admin login process is a secure access point for system administrators. Admins sign in from the admin login page using their credentials, and once authenticated they are redirected to the admin dashboard. From there they can review staff approvals, manage the clearance period, monitor document workflows, and access system health and audit areas.

## Main system steps

### Step 1: Student registration
A student creates an account and fills in their details. The system validates the required data and confirms their identity through the registration process.

### Step 2: Profile setup
Before document submission, the student must upload:
- passport photograph
- digital signature

This is required to activate the form upload feature.

### Step 3: Clearance form submission
The student uploads the official clearance form as a PDF. The system checks that the document is valid and within the active clearance period.

### Step 4: Bursar approval
The uploaded clearance form is sent to the Bursar for review. The Bursar checks the document and either approves or rejects it.

### Step 5: Auditor approval
If the Bursar approves, the form goes to the Auditor for the final clearance approval. Once approved, the signed clearance form becomes available for download.

### Step 6: Course registration form
The student can also register courses for the semester. The course registration is reviewed by the Head of Department (HOD), who approves or rejects it.

### Step 7: Download signed documents
Once a document is approved, the student can view details and download the signed PDF copy.

### Step 8: Admin monitoring
Admins can track all submitted forms, monitor approval progress, manage staff access, and control the clearance submission window.

## Simple user journey

### For students
1. Register account
2. Complete profile setup
3. Upload clearance form
4. Wait for faculty review
5. Check approval status
6. Register courses
7. Wait for HOD approval
8. Download final signed documents

### For staff
1. Sign in to the dashboard
2. Open review queue
3. Review the submitted document
4. Approve or reject with comments
5. Sign if required
6. Continue to the next assignment

### For admin
1. Login to admin portal
2. Review staff requests
3. Open student records and document status
4. Manage clearance periods
5. Monitor system health and audit logs

## Notes
This project is designed for a university environment and focuses on secure, digital approval workflows. It reduces paperwork, improves transparency, and speeds up the approval process for both students and staff.
